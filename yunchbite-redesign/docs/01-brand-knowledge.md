# Yunch Bite: brand knowledge

Source: a full harvest of the current site, 44 pages, 2026-11-04. Exact copy, screenshots and assets are in `research/current-site/`, and every asset is listed in `research/current-site/assets/manifest.json`. The client's instruction is to take facts **as they appear on the site** for now and correct them later.

> **The production domain isn't serving the site.** `https://yunchbite.com/` returns a 114-byte page that only redirects to `/lander`, and its sitemap lists nothing else. The real site exists only on the Vercel preview host. So right now Google can't see the brand site at all. Launching the new site on the real domain is the single biggest SEO win available.

### Audiences (the "Why Yunch Bite" page names four)

1. **Health-conscious consumers.** Clean ingredients, olive-oil roasted, protein and fibre.
2. **Parents.** Tiffin boxes, after-school snacks, "no junk, no worries".
3. **Retailers.** High-demand category, range of pack sizes, margins, marketing support.
4. **Distributors.** The contact page has a "Partnership with us?" form for stocking and distribution.

With no shop on the site, **the partnership/distribution enquiry is the main business conversion**. Consumers are steered towards flavour discovery and social media.

### Claims (as on the site)

- Roasted in **olive oil** ("virgin olive oil" in some copy)
- **Zero palm oil** (about page)
- **No preservatives or additives** · "no artificial colours, flavours or preservatives"
- **High in protein** · **rich in fibre**
- **Gluten-free** ("naturally gluten-free")
- "Clean, safe & hygienic" facilities

> **Claim conflict, still open:** the peri-peri ingredients list **Citric Acid (INS 330)**, which conflicts with "no additives". Nutrition values also differ between pages: the benefits page shows roasted makhana per 100 g at **347 kcal, 0.1 g fat**, while the peri-peri product page shows **428 kcal, 14.2 g fat**. Those are plain makhana vs seasoned and oil-roasted, but the site doesn't say so. To be corrected later with the client.

**URL clean-up (proposed, needs approval):** the sitemap lists `/categories/*`, but the site's own links go to the misspelt `/catergries/*`, and `/products/{makhana,popcorn,dry-fruits}` duplicates them (with canonical tags pointing at `/`). The proposal is to keep `/categories/*` as the real URLs and **301-redirect** `/catergries/*` and `/products/{category}` to them. The sitemap stays the same and nothing breaks. Since the production domain isn't serving the site today, almost no ranking is at risk.

## 6. Asset gaps for the cut-out direction

These go into the asset request list in the site overview:

1. **Pack cut-outs:** a transparent PNG for **every SKU and format** (jar, pillow, zipper) of the **current real** packaging, front-facing plus one ¾ angle, ≥2000 px tall. This matters most.
2. **Logo** as vector (SVG/AI/PDF), plus a white version.
3. **Makhana Buddy:** more poses, transparent, high resolution (only one small transparent pose exists today).
4. **Ingredient cut-outs:** makhana kernels, chilli, pudina leaves, pepper, salt crystals, cheese, berries, popcorn, almonds, cashews.
5. **Packaging fonts and colour codes**, if the packaging uses something other than Montserrat / Roboto Condensed.
