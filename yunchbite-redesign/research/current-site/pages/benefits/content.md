## Wholesome Benefits for a Better You

Yunch Bite snacks are more than just tasty – they are made to support your everyday wellness. Here's what makes them a smart choice:

High in Protein

Helps build and repair body tissues, supports muscle health and keeps you energized.

Rich in Fiber

Supports healthy digestion, keeps you full for longer and promotes gut health.

Roasted in Olive Oil

Olive oil is rich in healthy fats and antioxidants that support heart health and overall wellness.

No Preservatives or Additives

We keep it real and clean. No artificial colors, flavors, or preservatives – ever.

Gluten Free

Naturally gluten free snacks that are easy to digest and suitable for most diets.

Great Taste, Guilt-Free

Crunchy, flavorful and satisfying – without the extra calories or unhealthy oils.

Goodness in Every Bite

Our makhana, nuts and popcorn are packed with essential nutrients like iron, magnesium, potassium and antioxidants that help support immunity, bone health and overall well-being.
