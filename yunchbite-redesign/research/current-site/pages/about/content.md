# About Us

Yunch Bite is an Indian FMCG healthy-snacking brand founded on **18 October, 2023** by **Mr. Nitin Rathi, Founder & Managing Director**.  
Yunch bite was created with one simple belief: “Healthy snacking should never mean compromising on great taste.”  
We create delicious, crunchy and thoughtfully crafted snacks that bring together taste, quality, variety and better snacking choices, making enjoyable snacking accessible to people of all ages.

### [Button: Our Story]

### Our Story

Yunch Bite began with a simple observation: while many snacks focus on health, consumers often have to compromise when it comes to taste.

Our founder wanted to change that.  
The idea was to create a brand where people could enjoy the **crunch, flavour and excitement of a great snack** while also making a more conscious choice about what they eat. Makhana became the starting point of this journey and continues to be the **hero category of Yunch Bite.**

The name **Yunch Bite** draws inspiration from the ideas of **Yan** and **Chi**, representing energy and vitality. This philosophy is reflected in our identity and our belief that everyday snacking should contribute to an energetic, enjoyable lifestyle.

As Yunch Bite grew, our range expanded from Makhana to Popcorn and Dry Fruits, giving consumers more choices across flavours, preferences and occasions.

At the heart of everything remains one belief: **Better snacking should be delicious, not boring.**

### [Button: Our Journey]

### Our Journey

Yunch Bite's journey began with Makhana and a desire to create something different in the healthy-snacking space. We started by focusing on developing flavourful Makhana that could deliver both **great taste and a satisfying crunch.** As we understood consumer preferences and the growing demand for accessible snacking options, we expanded into **Popcorn and Dry Fruits.** Today, our journey continues with a focus on developing new flavours, expanding our product range and making Yunchbite available to more consumers across India.

Every step is guided by the same objective: Create better snacks. Make them delicious. Make them accessible. And this is only the beginning of the Yunch Bite journey.

### [Button: Our Mission & Vision]

Our Mission & Vision

Our mission is **to make better snacking delicious, accessible and enjoyable for everyone**.

We want to create snacks that people genuinely look forward to eating – without feeling that choosing a better option means giving up taste. From children and families to teenagers, working professionals, fitness-conscious consumers and seniors, we want Yunch Bite to become a part of everyday snacking.

Our vision is **to build Yunch Bite into one of India's most trusted healthy-snacking FMCG brands**, recognised for **great taste, quality, trust, innovation and variety**.

As we grow, we aim to expand beyond our current categories and gradually build a broader FMCG food portfolio while staying rooted in the principles that created Yunchbite. Our long-term goal is to encourage more people to choose better snacks without feeling that health comes at the cost of enjoyment.

Great taste. Better choices. A healthier way to snack.

### [Button: Our Product Philosophy]

Our Product Philosophy

At Yunch Bite, we believe that the quality of a snack begins with what goes into it.

Our philosophy is built around **quality ingredients, distinctive flavours, satisfying crunch and consistent product experiences**. We focus on creating products that consumers can enjoy for their taste while making a more conscious everyday snacking choice.

What Makes Yunch Bite Different?

Taste Without Compromise

We don't want customers to choose Yunch Bite simply because they think it is a healthier option. We want them to choose it because **it genuinely tastes great**. Our focus is on creating distinctive flavours that make every bite enjoyable and memorable.

Quality Comes First

Quality is one of the most important principles behind Yunch Bite. From our core ingredients to flavour development, manufacturing and packaging, we focus on maintaining standards that allow customers to trust the products they purchase.

Thoughtfully Crafted Flavours

We believe healthy snacking should still be exciting. Our flavour range is designed to offer different taste experiences – from spicy and tangy to fresh and savoury – while maintaining our focus on quality ingredients.

Variety for Every Preference

Our portfolio currently includes:

Makhana

Peri Peri Paradise • Chatpata Masala • Pudina Masala • Himalayan Salt & Pepper • Black Salt • Raw Makhana

Dry Fruits

Roasted Cashews • Raw Cashews • Roasted Almonds • Raw Almonds

Popcorn

Salted • Butter • Pudina

Makhana remains our hero category, while we continue exploring new flavours, products and snack categories.

Better Snacking for Everyone

We don't believe better snacking should be limited to one age group or lifestyle. Yunch Bite is being built for children, families, teenagers, working professionals, fitness-conscious consumers and seniors – essentially anyone looking for an enjoyable snack that fits better into their everyday choices.

Our Quality Promise

Our promise is simple:

- Quality Ingredients
- Consistent Taste
- Carefully Developed Flavours
- Hygienic Manufacturing
- Quality Packaging
- Continuous Improvement

We want every Yunch Bite product to deliver the same feeling:

“This tastes great, and I can trust what I'm eating.”

### [Button: Our Founder]

Our Founder

Mr. Nitin Rathi

Founder & Managing Director

Yunch Bite was founded by **Mr. Nitin Rathi** with the vision of creating a brand that brings together **great taste and better snacking choices**.

His belief is straightforward: consumers should not have to choose between something that tastes delicious and something that fits better into a conscious lifestyle. That belief became the foundation of Yunch Bite and continues to influence the way we approach **products, flavours, quality and innovation**.

Under his direction, Yunch Bite is focused on building more than a snack brand – it is building a long-term FMCG brand based on trust, quality and enjoyable everyday snacking.
