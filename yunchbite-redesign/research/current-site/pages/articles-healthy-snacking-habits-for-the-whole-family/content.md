## Building a Healthy Pantry Together

Children are naturally drawn to crunch, vibrant flavors, and fun shapes. Instead of banning snacks, the secret lies in pantry swaps: replacing artificial fried namkeens with flavorful peri-peri and chatpata makhanas.

When kids enjoy snacks that celebrate natural crunch without sugar crashes or sluggishness, wholesome eating becomes a joyful family tradition rather than a chore.
