## 100% Whole Grain Goodness

Popcorn is one of the few snacks that is 100% unprocessed whole grain. The outer hull contains concentrated ferulic acid, an antioxidant that defends cells against oxidative stress.

Because of its expansive puffed volume, popcorn satisfies cravings with far fewer calories per bowl compared to biscuits or potato crisps.

## Clean Popcorn, Zero Artificial Additives

YunchBite crafts classic salted and butter popcorn using non-GMO kernels, popping them to fluffy golden perfection with pure rock salt and clean aromatic seasoning.
