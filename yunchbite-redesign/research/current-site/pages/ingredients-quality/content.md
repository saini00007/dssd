## Product Quality You Can Trust

Every Yunch Bite product goes through multiple quality checks to ensure you get consistent taste, freshness and nutrition in every bite.

Our makhana, nuts and popcorn are roasted / popped in premium quality olive oil – never fried. Olive oil is known for its heart-healthy fats and natural antioxidants, making our snacks a smarter and healthier choice.

| Pudina Masala | Makhana (Fox Nuts), Olive Oil, Pudina Seasoning [Iodized Salt, Mint Powder, Black Salt, Cumin Powder, Coriander Powder, Black Pepper, Spices & Condiments, Acidity Regulator (INS 330)]. |
| Himalayan Salt & Pepper | Makhana (Fox Nuts), Olive Oil, Himalayan Pink Salt, Black Pepper, Spices & Condiments. |
| Black Salt | Makhana (Fox Nuts), Olive Oil, Black Salt, Cumin Powder, Spices & Condiments, Acidity Regulator (INS 330). |

All our makhana is roasted in olive oil – never fried.
